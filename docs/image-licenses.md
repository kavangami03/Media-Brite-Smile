# Image licence record (Media Brite Smile website)

**Purpose:** proof of where every photo on Media Brite Smile website came from, and under which licence. If anyone raises a copyright question about an image, this file shows the source page, the date and the licence for each one.

- **Sources used:** only free libraries whose licences allow commercial use with no attribution required: Pexels, Pixabay, Unsplash (never "Unsplash+"). Nothing comes from Google Images, Pinterest, social media or paid stock sites.
- **Downloaded from:** the libraries' official image servers. Files may be resized and compressed for the web, which all three licences allow.
- **Record started:** 2026-11-16.

## 1. Licence terms (in each library's own words)

**Pexels License:** https://www.pexels.com/license/
- "All photos and videos on Pexels are free to use." "Attribution is not required." "You can modify the photos and videos from Pexels."
- Not allowed: identifiable people "in a bad light or in a way that is offensive"; selling "unaltered copies"; implying "endorsement of your product by people or brands on the imagery"; use "as part of your trade-mark … business name or service mark"; redistributing on other stock or wallpaper platforms.

**Pixabay Content License:** https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/
- "Use Content for free." "Use Content without having to attribute the author." "Modify or adapt Content into new works."
- Not allowed: selling or distributing content "on a Standalone basis"; immoral or illegal use, especially with recognisable people; recognisable trademarks used commercially for goods and services; use as a trade mark or business name; misleading use.

**Unsplash License:** https://unsplash.com/license
- "All images can be downloaded and used for free", for "commercial and non-commercial purposes". Credit is optional.
- Not allowed: selling images "without significant modification"; compiling images "to replicate a similar or competing service". Unsplash+ images are paid and are not used.

## 2. Rules this site follows
- Photos are website illustrations only. They are never sold, printed for sale or uploaded to other stock sites.
- No stock photo is paired with a testimonial, quote, name or job title (no implied endorsement).
- No stock photo is used in the logo, brand mark or business name.
- Photos with readable third-party logos or signs are avoided.
- People are shown in a neutral or positive, professional way.
